Academic Workload Management System

Academic workload, worked out the same way for everyone.

AWMS brings teaching, service, supervision, releases and leave into one annual figure per faculty member. Your department's own rules drive the calculation, every number traces back to its source, and each year ends with a statement that faculty acknowledge and the chair signs off.

Features

Everything a department needs to plan, calculate and confirm workload

Live course data

Courses, sections and enrolment are mirrored from ROSI. Nobody retypes them, and nobody can edit them by accident.

Your policy, as rules

Write workload policy as conditions and effects, publish it as a version, and test changes in the policy simulator first.

One annual figure

Fall, Winter and Summer add up to a year. Releases and leave lower the target rather than making anyone look underloaded.

Assignment planning

Assign instructors section by section. Each assignment is checked against the person's annual load before it's saved.

Releases, leave and credits

Managed release types with caps and grant accounts, leave with FTE, teaching credits, service roles and supervision.

Annual sign-off

Publish a frozen statement, let faculty acknowledge it, and have the chair sign off. Every revision is kept.

Disputes that become fixes

Faculty flag the exact line. Staff open it in the editor, and the reply shows what changed, before and after.

One inbox, one checklist

Everything waiting on someone lands in one inbox, and a term checklist shows how far planning has got.

Annual sign-off

Close the year with a record everyone agreed to

  1. 1

    Staff publish

    The year is frozen from the latest calculation as a numbered revision. Recalculating with no change doesn't create a new one.

  2. 2

    Faculty acknowledge, or dispute

    They review the statement line by line on their dashboard. While a dispute is open, they can't acknowledge.

  3. 3

    The chair signs off

    Sign-off is blocked if figures have moved since publishing. Every sign-off is audited, and later changes need a new revision.

How it works

Facts from ROSI, decisions in AWMS

AWMS keeps the source of truth and the department's decisions apart, so a change to one never quietly overwrites the other.

  1. 1 · Source

    ROSI

    The university's system controls courses, sections, enrolment and terms. AWMS only reads from it.

  2. 2 · Mirror

    Read-only snapshot

    A synced copy with change detection. Sections removed in ROSI are marked inactive, never deleted.

  3. 3 · Governance

    AWMS decisions

    Assignments, releases, adjustments and sign-offs. This is the only layer anyone edits.

Each calculation is saved as a snapshot of the course data, the assignments and the published policy version it used. Months later, you can still show exactly how a number was reached.

Security and accountability

Built for university data

University sign-in
Supports UTORid single sign-on through Shibboleth.
Scoped access
Staff and chairs see only their own department. Faculty see only their own records.
Full audit trail
Publishing, sign-off, dispute replies and other changes are recorded with who and when.
Reproducible numbers
Calculations are tied to a published policy version and a data snapshot.
Modern password storage
Local accounts are hashed with Argon2id.
Safe error handling
Users see a generic message and a reference ID. Details stay in the server logs.
